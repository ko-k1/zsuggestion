# Code of Conduct

## Expected Behaviour

Treat participants with respect. Be constructive, assume good faith, respect
privacy, and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, doxxing, and personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project spaces and to other spaces when someone is
representing the project.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainer,
[@ko-k1](https://github.com/ko-k1), through GitHub. Do not open a public issue
about an incident.

Maintainers will review reports, protect reporter confidentiality where
practical, and take proportionate action.
